Privacy Policy for Tiny Detective

Effective Date: October 27, 2026 • Application: Tiny Detective

Welcome to Tiny Detective ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of children and families. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information.

1. Children's Privacy & COPPA / GDPR-K Compliance

Tiny Detective is designed for children and families. We strictly comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), General Data Protection Regulation for Children (GDPR-K), and Google Play’s Designed for Families policies:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do NOT collect, store, or ask for any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical locations from children.
  • No Account Registration Required: Children can play the game without creating an account or logging in.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: Tiny Detective is 100% ad-free. We do not display third-party advertisements.

2. Local Data Storage & Gameplay Progress

All gameplay data—including earned stars, unlocked levels, badges, and settings—is stored locally on the user's device using Android DataStore Preferences. This data never leaves the device and is not transmitted to external servers.

3. In-App Purchases & Parental Gate

Tiny Detective offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock additional educational seasons:

  • Parental Gate: All purchase and restore flows are protected by a mandatory "Grown-Ups Only" Math Gate to prevent accidental taps by children.
  • Google Play Billing: In-App purchases are securely processed directly through Google Play’s official Billing Library. We do not collect or store any financial or credit card information.

4. Device Permissions

Tiny Detective does not require or request sensitive permissions such as location access, camera, contacts, microphone, or external storage.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
